Good to know

How do I order?

Choose food from Menu, open Cart, enter your name and group, and place your order.

Where do I collect my order?

Collect your order at the JIHC canteen. Show your order number to the staff.

Can I pay online?

This project does not collect online payments. Ask the canteen staff about payment when collecting your order.
